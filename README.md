# 📷 Creepy Photo Booth
*A totally normal camera app. Nothing weird here.* 👻

Take a photo and it looks normal. Open it, and a shadow figure slowly fades in.

---

## 📲 Install on your iPhone (same GitHub method as before)

### Part 1 – Put it on GitHub (on a computer is easiest)
1. Go to **github.com** and sign in.
2. Click the **+** (top right) → **New repository**.
3. Name it: `creepy-photo-booth`
4. Pick **Public** → click **Create repository**.
5. Click the **"uploading an existing file"** link.
6. Unzip `creepy-photo-booth.zip` on your computer.
7. Open the unzipped folder and **drag everything inside it** onto the GitHub page:
   `index.html, app.js, haunt.js, style.css, manifest.json, sw.js, README.md` **and the `icons` folder**.
8. Scroll down → click **Commit changes**.

### Part 2 – Turn on GitHub Pages
1. In your repo, click **Settings** (the ⚙️ tab).
2. On the left, click **Pages**.
3. Under **Branch**, choose **main** and **/ (root)** → click **Save**.
4. Wait about 1–2 minutes and refresh. You'll see a link like:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/creepy-photo-booth/`

### Part 3 – Put it on your Home Screen
1. Open that link in **Safari** on your iPhone (it has to be Safari).
2. Tap **Allow** when it asks to use the camera.
3. Tap the **Share** button (square with the arrow ↑).
4. Scroll down → tap **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
5. It shows up as **"Camera"** with a normal-looking camera icon. 😈
6. Open it once while you're online. After that it works **fully offline**.

## 🎛️ How to use it
| Thing | What it does |
|---|---|
| **⌃ (top middle)** | Secret Options: choose the entity, intensity, frequency, reveal effect |
| **Shutter** | Takes the photo (and quietly haunts it) |
| **Thumbnail (bottom left)** | Opens your photos. The thumbnail always looks innocent |
| **SQUARE / PHOTO** | Photo shape (you can also swipe on the viewfinder) |
| **LIBRARY** | Haunt a photo that's already in your Camera Roll |
| **⚡ / ⏱ / 1× 2** | Flash, 3s/10s timer, zoom |

**In the photo viewer:** ⬆️ Save/Share (pick **Save Image** to put it in Photos) · 🔄 Summon a different figure · 👁 Compare with the original · 🗑 Delete. Swipe left/right to browse, swipe down to close.

### Entities
The Tall Man · The Hooded One · The Girl · The Peeker · The Hands · The Watcher · Wall Shadow · 🎲 Random

### Tips for max creepiness
- Point the camera at **dark doorways, hallways, windows at night, or dim corners**. The app looks for dark areas and puts figures there.
- Use **Subtle** for "wait… what's that?" and **Obvious** for screaming.
- Set Frequency to **Sometimes** so your friend can't figure out the pattern.

## 🔄 Updating later
Upload the new files to the same repo (replace the old ones). On your iPhone, open the app twice to pick up the new version.

## 🗂️ Where are my photos?
They're saved privately inside the app on your phone. Nothing gets uploaded anywhere. Use **Save/Share → Save Image** to copy one into your Photos app.
